Godot Destruction Core – Godot 4 2D destruction, collapse & chain reaction kit

Blow it up, watch it fall, let it burn: deterministic 2D destruction for Godot 4.

Godot Destruction Core: deterministic 2D destruction for Godot 4 in typed GDScript: destructible cell terrain, structural collapse, chain reactions and fire. Free MIT Lite on GitHub (76 headless tests), Pro on itch.io (133 headless tests).

Get it

  • Lite (free, MIT): https://github.com/openclawaimac/godot-destruction-core-lite – Destructible cell terrain, damage shapes, single detonations and deterministic structural collapse with falling pieces, rubble and crush damage, plus a two-player demo. 76 tests.

Features

  • Destructible cell terrain: integer hit points, resistances, anchors, rubble; circle, rect, line and cell damage with falloff
  • Structural collapse with a resumable support check (no size cap), falling pieces, rubble and crush damage
  • DKStress (Pro): load-based shear and crush failure, budgeted sweeps, analysis-only heat map
  • DKChainReactor (Pro): fuses, merging, per-tick budget, runaway caps, barrels/triggers, explosive materials, one hitstop per chain
  • DKFire (Pro): deterministic spread, burn-out to ash/charcoal, burnt supports collapse, flames ride falling pieces and light barrels
  • DKMaskAdapter (Pro): alpha/palette images to terrain, mask carve stamps, grid to image
  • Debug overlays (Pro): chain graph, stress heat map, "why did this fall?" inspector, frame-budget HUD
  • DKCommandLog (Pro): record and replay to an identical hash with checkpoints, save/load
  • Per-player credit for every blast, collapse, chain and fire; SHA-256 state digest, snapshot/restore
  • Pure typed GDScript, no GDExtension; web builds of both demos

Not included

  • Cell-based, not per-pixel or rigid-body fracture; pieces do not collide in the air
  • No networking
  • Tested headless on Godot 4.7.2 and in headless Chrome; real browsers/devices not tested
